# Backup, independent deny witness và erasure

FW-RECOVERY-T1-001 · **DESIGN / NO BACKUP OR RESTORE EXECUTED**. [Schemas event/receipt/head](recovery-event.schema.json), [policy](security-policy.json), [storage delta](../../data/target-t1-security/README.md). T1 tin custodians; protocol ngăn lỗi/stale recovery và callers không đủ quyền, không cam kết chống operator có toàn quyền cố ý sửa mọi bản.

## 1. Backup thật gồm những gì

Daily consistent logical PostgreSQL custom export (`pg_dump -Fc` direction), verified migration/release/schema hashes, role/grant manifest không password, payload/object inventory nếu sau này có objects ngoài PG, environment/key **references** và verified recovery checkpoint ref. File exports lưu staging trên encrypted volume, never plaintext download URL. Dump failure không được đánh dấu successful snapshot. pg_dump có snapshot consistency cho một DB nhưng không tự backup roles/keys/externalfiles; backup principal cần đọc đủ dữ liệu và được tin, không `--enable-row-security` rồi vô tình chỉ backup một tenant. [PostgreSQL backup](https://www.postgresql.org/docs/current/backup.html), [pg_dump](https://www.postgresql.org/docs/18/app-pgdump.html).

Chọn restic native encrypted+authenticated repository, off-host TLS authenticated rest-server với **append-only source writer**; pruning/restore backend credential giữ ngoài sourcehost. [Rest-server](https://github.com/restic/rest-server), [restic format](https://restic.readthedocs.io/en/stable/100_references.html). Restic không dùng AES-GCM của auth envelopes; không tự đổi/encrypt lại repository bằng crypto tự viết. Repository password trên backup process có khả năng decrypt được tin; append-only quyền backend không biến process này thành không đọc được finance.

Daily job có filesystem/database lock tránh hai exports/pruners xung đột; provider/network I/O ngoài financial transactions. Snapshot only success sau full export+off-host receipt+inventory, timestamp server và destination-controlled receive time, không client/source forged time. Backup objects chỉ ciphertext, key capsule age riêng, không key private trong backup tự mở chính nó. Source PC/VM vừa chứa DB vừa chứa repo không là off-host. Personal PC có thể làm synthetic destination nhưng khi tắt/quota/networkfail phải overdue; real cần endpoint luôn đạt measured availability và alert ngoài VM. Destination chưa chọn/provision, không free-storage guarantee.

## 2. Retention và ransomware trade-off

7daily+4weekly là preferred recovery points **trong hard age34days**. Mỗi ngày custodian independently enumerate mọi snapshots/host/path/tag scopes, dựa trusted inventory/receive times, remove tất cả quá34days, rồi prune/repack unreferenced data trong24h; không chỉ `forget` làm mất metadata. `--keep-*` kết hợp OR và count có thể kéo dài khi schedule bị gián đoạn, nên không dùng shorthand7/4 như proof35days. [Restic retention/pruning](https://restic.readthedocs.io/en/stable/060_forget.html).

Future timestamp/unexpected snapshot/host/path/tag không được kéo dài retention hoặc ép bỏ last-good snapshot từ poisoned labels. Append-only source không có pruning credentials; custodian xét trusted catalog và incident state, không tự tin nguồn host sau compromise. Nếu chỉ còn snapshot quá hạn, không giữ vô hạn âm thầm: privacy deadline và recovery availability cùng fail gate, cần incident handling; không báo RPO PASS. Immutable-provider retention/soft-delete/versioning/offline exports phải kiểm kê và expiry tương thích; nếu không thể xóa trong35days thì REAL gate chưa đạt, không giả đã purge.

Target RPO24h/RTO24h chưa đo, single-host không HA/zero loss; acknowledged transaction/receipt sau last good backup có thể mất khi restore. Native ACK replay horizon7d/1000items là bounded candidate cần ARCH-09/10 kiểm, không bảo đảm reconstruct toàn bộ financial history. Missing receipt sau restore không tự cho phép repost/new event key; investigation/coverage gap theo API002.

## 3. Independent deny witness: tránh hồi sinh dữ liệu/quyền

Local recovery_journal trong DB backup không đủ: backup cũ có thể chưa chứa erase/revoke mới. Chọn independent witness ngoài finance VM/DB/backup snapshot với own append store+fsync và signing key; lưu **opaque deny metadata**, không email/amount/bank raw/token/prompt/SQL/private position. Đây là recovery control endpoint nhỏ, không một financial microservice/queue authority mới. Synthetic witness có thể chạy separate test process, nhưng cùnghost test không chứng minh off-host independence; real endpoint/key/capacity chưa có.

Mỗi identity/workspace/device có stable random recovery_token32bytes, generated once trước real backup; không hash email hoặc regenerate khi restore. Target lock cấp recovery_control_epoch riêng, khác auth_epoch/deviceEpoch/recovery_epoch. Event schema có eventId/environmentId/scopeKind/targetToken/epoch/kind/createdAt/producerKeyId, no arbitrary metadata. Producer signature Ed2551964bytes raw base64url trên UTF-8 RFC8785 event; eventDigest=SHA256 canonical bytes. [RFC8032](https://www.rfc-editor.org/rfc/rfc8032.html). Exact library/cross-runtime crypto tests NOT RUN.

Witness verifies registered producer key/environment, event shape/signature, deny-only kind and target control epoch; unique(environment,eventId) same digest→same receipt, different digest→conflict. Append serialize, fsync event+sequence+hashchain and durable head before signed receipt. No source DELETE/update/permit grant. `recordHash=SHA256(JCS({environmentId,sequence,previousRecordHash,eventId,eventDigest}))`, genesis previoushash64zeros. Receipt signature covers **all receipt fields except signature**; witness key is independent. DB witness_receipt alone is not proof of current head. Concurrent publication may arrive out of target-epoch order: accept each distinct valid deny event, preserve its immutable epoch and advance the target floor with max(current, event.epoch). A late lower-epoch deny never lowers the floor or grants access. The witness stream sequence remains strictly serialized; it is distinct from each target control epoch. A conflicting event for the same target/epoch is rejected and investigated.

Latest-head query từ authorized restore client gửi fresh random challengeNonce32bytes; signed head response binds nonce, environmentId, current sequence/recordHash, issuedAt/keyId. Require timestamp±60s và known public key history, fetch/verify contiguous chain through this head, not một old signed prefix. Witness crashes before receipt/after append: replay exact eventId/digest returns durable receipt; if head and log inconsistent repair/quarantine before any ACK. Unavailable/stale/wrong env/gap/duplicate-seq/conflicting head→no restore reopening. Routine key rollover preserves signed public trust chain; key loss hoặc ambiguity không fallback về local journal.

## 4. Revoke/erase publication không network trong DB transaction

1. Validate current actor/object/reauth, original command key/digest. Under short target transaction **deny locally immediately**: revoke credentials/device/binding as applicable, fence current effects; for erasure set workspace access_state=erasing/identity suspended, freeze children. Allocate recovery_control_epoch, append immutable journal event and recovery_delivery_state=prepared atomically. No external HTTP while locks held.
2. Coordinator reads immutable signed event and appends off-host using exact eventId/digest. Multiple retries/concurrent publishers are harmless idempotent deny append; no new general financial dispatcher/lease layer. Witness outage keeps local denial effective; do not undo revoke to regain availability.
3. Verified durable receipt→second short transaction mark anchored and command recovery-safe completion. Only then successful revoke/reset completion is marked recoverably complete/replayable. Crash before second commit resumes exact event/key; no duplicate effect/event. Identity erase fans out a frozen child workspace request/event list before dropping parent data.
4. Local denial committed but publication unknown: existing API002503 `OUTCOME_UNKNOWN`, outcome=unknown or committed **local command** with safe correlation as appropriate; no `not_committed` and no success bool claiming anchored completion. Client preserves original key/input, clears own credentials on explicit logout regardless of network, displays pending/unknown; do not automatically mint new key/regrant. Credential issuer final result/replay body is written only after required anchor; recovery_delivery_state tracks pending stage, not generic finance result cache.

Guarantee scope: **successful recovery-safe completion ACK** requires independent durable deny intent; an unacknowledged prepared local request may be lost inside DB RPO and must remain unknown after failure. Do not claim zero loss for in-flight actions. Auth self logout local clearing still works when witness unavailable. A generic committed outcome is not itself witness proof. No erasure “all copies deleted” ACK until full erasure completion criteria below.

Single-session logout/revoke can conservatively record identity-scope deny control without revoking unrelated online sessions; restore invalidates all restored credentials anyway. Per-binding revoke is local narrow state change, and restore disables **all** old bindings before any upload, so it cannot revive an old binding through reauth. Device revoke records device deny; no permission-positive events are accepted by witness. Existing scope auth/object versions remain separate, never replace them with the recovery control counter.

Password reset may commit the new password hash and local revocations before publication, but the identity remains suspended until its reset event is anchored. Finalization locks and rechecks the current target epoch, erasure state and request identity; an older reset completion cannot clear a newer denial, erase request or recovery guard. A retry may finish that same reset only, never issue a session or undo a later revoke. This state-machine behavior still needs runtime concurrency evidence.

## 5. Locked restore runbook

1. Declare recovery incident, **fence old host/DB/network writer** and prove it stopped serving; if cannot fence, no new writer/opening. Single-host/manual recovery, no implicit HA or split-brain election.
2. Restore into isolated environment with providers/model/mail/uploads/financial workers/public access **disabled**. Verify artifact/repo/migration hashes, ciphertext integrity, key capsule availability, proper environment/role mapping. Build schema from pinned migrations then data-only allowlisted restore direction; no arbitrary untrusted dump SQL/root execution. Dump schema content is not trusted merely because it has a hash.
3. Fetch nonce-bound latest witness head independently, verify chain/events/key history; correlate stable target tokens. Apply every erased workspace/identity deny **before any finance reads/exports**, including children. Unknown token mapping/legacy backup without tokens→quarantine, not guess target from email. Rebuild recovery_control_epoch floors without replacing domain epoch semantics.
4. Invalidate all restored browser/native/admin sessions, refresh/access/collector credentials, challenges, replay ciphertext, offline recovery codes, device grants/bindings. Increment appropriate domain restore/auth/device fences; new server session/replay/CSRF keys. identities.recovery_reset_required=true: restored old password hash cannot itself log in. Require fresh email reset through currently verified contact, then new signin/enrollment; current API has no email-change feature, any future contact-change API must add independent current-contact recovery binding before supported restore. If contact validity uncertain, manual identity proof or deny, never old-mail fallback.
5. Admin factors reprovision through trusted infrastructure runbook; no restored MFA/session backdoor. Owner codes regenerated only after fresh auth/reauth. Old outbox/device owner/capture identity is immutable; reconcile/Review/gap, not retarget old items to fresh owner epoch.
6. Verify ledger/projection/history invariants, tenant/Ops/profile grants and denied-target scans, restore catalog/roles/secrets/egress controls, run synthetic AUTH/SEC/domain cases. Ready only after witness/protocol verification and incident sign-off; missing latest witness/key/backup/deletion inventory→remain LOCKED. Emit minimal recovery report, not finance or secret dumps.

## 6. Erasure completion

States requested→denied→anchored→active_purged→backup_pending→completed, failures remain denied. Owner-authorized privileged runbook/profile, **not app-admin button**; no new public erase API invented in ARCH-07. Existing “source revoke”/“delete draft” không erasure toàn workspace. Identity parent freezes all owned workspace child requests while preventing createWorkspace race; parent cannot complete before all children.

Active purge includes ledger/history/Plan–Goals/drafts/chat/tools/receipts/evidence/import/cache/export and private source/identity/credentials by checked dependency order. Immutable guards remain for ordinary runtime; narrow authorized erasure procedure is explicit exception, not blanket DELETE grant to retention. Keep only opaque deny/control tombstones, no erased-owner FK. originalrequest+35days deadline does not reset on retry. Under the same target/ownership lock, a repeated request with a different command key resumes or references the existing erasure workflow and keeps the earliest accepted original deadline; it cannot start a new 35-day clock or omit existing child/copy obligations. Completed requires verified active purge **and** all inventoried server-controlled backup/export copies removed/pruned, not merely hidden/expired/snapshotforgotten. Off-host policies/softdeleted copies can block completion; alert overdue, don't silently extend.

Retain deny records>=90days and until all relevant recoverable copies expired+7day native replay horizon, whichever later. Offline owner device/user-downloaded exports/provider retained logs outside server control cannot be remotely guaranteed erased; label server completion scope and unknown device status honestly. No instant per-owner cryptoshred claim: restic repository key is shared, encrypted volume does not remove an owner from old snapshots.
